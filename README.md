Sizzle Rush — an original 2D cooking game for make.fun's "Make the best cooking game" bounty ($250, top 10 paid). You are the night-shift chef of a midnight diner: tickets keep coming, so grab ingredients from the crates, chop them on the board, fry them on the stove, plate the dish to match the ticket, and serve it at the window before the customer's patience runs out. One 150-second shift, combos, and a rush hour at the end.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Play with keyboard (WASD/arrows to move, E to interact, hold E on the chopping board, P to pause, R to restart) or mouse (click to walk, click a station to use it). No network, no build step — plain static files under `game/`.
